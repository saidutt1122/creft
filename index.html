How it works

Three steps. Zero tab switching.

No more opening 10 tabs, trying different keywords on each platform. Creft gets what you mean the first time.

🧠
01

Describe what you're imagining

Type it like you'd explain it to a creative director. "Dark luxury Diwali campaign with gold tones" works. So does "a sound that feels like floating through space." No technical keywords needed.

⚡
02

Creft searches everywhere at once

Our AI reads the mood, style, tone and context behind your words — then simultaneously searches Pexels, Freesound, Behance, Google Fonts, Sketchfab and every other connected source.

📋
03

Save, moodboard, and present

Save assets to project boards, organise by section, upload your own work, and share a clean link with your client. No account needed on their end.
