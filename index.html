How it works

Three steps. Zero tab switching.

No more opening 10 tabs, trying different keywords on each site, hoping something matches. Creft gets what you mean the first time.

01

Describe what you're imagining

Type it like you'd explain it to a friend. "A dark rainy Tokyo street with neon" is a valid search. So is "a sound that feels like floating through space." No technical keywords needed.

02

Creft understands your intent

Our AI reads the mood, style, tone and context behind your words — then simultaneously searches YouTube, Instagram, Pexels, Freesound, and every other connected source at once.

03

Download, save, or moodboard

Free assets download in one click. Premium items redirect to the original source. Save anything into moodboards. Everything without leaving Creft.
